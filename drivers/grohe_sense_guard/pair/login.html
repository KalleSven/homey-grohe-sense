Grohe Ondus Login

Enter your Grohe Ondus Refresh Token to connect your Grohe Sense Guard.
How to get your Refresh Token:
  1. Open your browser and developer tools (F12 → Network tab).
  2. Visit: idp2-apigw.cloud.grohe.com/v3/iot/oidc/login
  3. Log in to your GROHE account.
  4. Look in the network tab for the failed request starting with ondus://.../token?....
  5. Open a new browser tab, paste that URL and replace ondus:// with https://.
  6. Copy the value inside "refresh_token": "..." and paste it below.
